Point. Scan. Understand.

Peshat reads text from your camera and translates it in 36 languages — entirely on your device. No account. No cloud. No tracking beyond what the ad SDK declares.

Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

Google Play listing is live. App Store URL will be enabled once the numeric App ID is assigned at submission.

What it does

On-device translation

Optical character recognition and translation run locally. Your scanned text and images never leave your phone.

36 languages

From Arabic and Amharic to Vietnamese and Korean — including right-to-left scripts, with correct text direction.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no profile. Open the app and scan.

Works offline

After first launch, translation works without a network connection. The only online component is the ad SDK.
