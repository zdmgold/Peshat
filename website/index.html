What Peshat does

Scan with your camera

Point the camera at a sign, menu, label or page. Peshat reads clear Latin-script text and translates it in seconds. For the most accurate translation, use Type text.

Import a file

Bring in a photo or a text file from your phone and translate the text it contains.

Type or paste text

Have text already? Type or paste it in and translate it, no camera needed.

59 translation languages

Translate between 59 languages. Peshat can detect the language of the text for you. Text in other writing systems, such as Arabic, Cyrillic, Chinese or Tamil, can be typed or pasted in.

App menus in 48 languages

The app itself speaks 48 languages, so the menus and messages read naturally in yours.

Copy, share and export

Copy a translation, share it, or export it as plain text or a PDF.

Recent scans

Your last scans are kept in a history so you can open them again. They stay on your phone, and you can clear them at any time.

Light, dark or system theme

Choose the look that suits you, or let Peshat follow your phone's setting.

Gentle reminders

Optional reminders if you haven't used Peshat for a few days. Switch them off whenever you like.

Built on your device

Private by design

Text recognition and translation run on your device. Your images are not uploaded and your scans are not sent to a server.

Works offline

Once a language pack has been received, translating that language needs no connection. Network use is limited to model downloads, fonts, ads and store purchases.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no profile. Open the app and scan.

Good to know

Peshat is honest about what it can and cannot do.

Machine translation

Translations are produced automatically. They are good for understanding the meaning of text, but they can be imperfect, so double-check anything important such as medical, legal or safety information.

A language pack is received once

The first time you use a language, Peshat needs an internet connection to receive its language pack. After that it works offline. A weak connection can make this step slow.

Image quality matters

Recognition works best with clear, well-lit, in-focus text. Blurry, curved or very stylised lettering may be read incorrectly.
