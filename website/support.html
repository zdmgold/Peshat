Frequently asked

Does Peshat send my scanned text anywhere?

No. Optical character recognition and translation both run entirely on your device. The app never sends your scanned content anywhere. Network activity is limited to downloading translation models and fonts, loading ads through the AdMob SDK, and the store when you buy or restore.

Why does the app ask for camera permission?

Peshat reads text through your device's camera. The permission is only used while the scanner is open. You can revoke it at any time in your device settings; the app will simply refuse to scan.

Why does iOS ask to track me?

The ad SDK (Google AdMob) may use your advertising identifier to serve personalized ads. If you decline, ads still appear but are non-personalized. The app itself does not track you across other apps or websites.

I bought the ad-removal. How do I restore it on a new device?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchase. The purchase is tied to the Apple ID or Google account you used at checkout. If the restore fails, ensure you are signed in to the same account.

Which languages are supported?

Peshat translates between 59 languages. The app's menus are available in 48: Amharic, Arabic, Bengali, Bulgarian, Burmese, Catalan, Chinese (Simplified), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Filipino, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Gujarati, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Khmer, Korean, Lao, Malay, Marathi, Nepali, Norwegian, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Punjabi, Romanian, Russian, Sinhala, Slovak, Spanish, Swahili, Swedish, Tamil, Telugu, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu and Vietnamese.

Which writing can the camera read?

Camera scanning and imported photos are read in Latin script (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Vietnamese and others), and in Devanagari (Hindi, Marathi), Bengali, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Text in other writing systems, such as Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew, Cyrillic, Greek, Thai, Gujarati, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu or Georgian, cannot be read from a photo, and Peshat shows no text rather than a wrong reading. You can type or paste it in and Peshat will translate it.

Can it translate text that mixes languages?

Yes. Text with words from two languages, such as English with Hindi words, or a sign with one line in English and one in Hindi, is translated language by language, so the English stays English and the other language is translated. This works when the text is typed or pasted, and in photos in the scripts the camera can read.

Does translation work offline?

Yes, once the translation models for both languages have been downloaded. Each language's model is downloaded once, on first use, then cached on the device. After that, translations between languages you have already used work without a network connection.

Why is the OCR wrong sometimes?

OCR accuracy depends on lighting, camera focus, and text clarity. Handwritten or highly stylized text may not be recognized. Try good, even lighting, hold the camera steady, and keep the text flat and in focus. You can also import a photo or file instead.

Can I submit a translation correction?

Not yet, but it is on the roadmap. If you spot a systematic error in a specific language pair, email us with the source text, the target language, and what the correct translation should be.
