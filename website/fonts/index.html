Fonts

These font files are downloaded by the Peshat app the first time you export a PDF containing Chinese, Japanese or Korean text. They are then kept on your device.

  • NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf (Chinese)
  • NotoSansJP-Regular.ttf (Japanese)
  • NotoSansKR-Regular.ttf (Korean)
