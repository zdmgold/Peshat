Fonts

These font files are downloaded by the Peshat app the first time you export a PDF containing Bengali, Georgian, Gujarati, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Chinese, Japanese or Korean text. They are then kept on your device.

  • NotoSansBengali-Regular.ttf (Bengali)
  • NotoSansGeorgian-Regular.ttf (Georgian)
  • NotoSansGujarati-Regular.ttf (Gujarati)
  • NotoSansKannada-Regular.ttf (Kannada)
  • NotoSansTamil-Regular.ttf (Tamil)
  • NotoSansTelugu-Regular.ttf (Telugu)
  • NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf (Chinese)
  • NotoSansJP-Regular.ttf (Japanese)
  • NotoSansKR-Regular.ttf (Korean)

Noto Sans fonts are © The Noto Project Authors and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The Chinese, Japanese and Korean files are subsets of the originals.
