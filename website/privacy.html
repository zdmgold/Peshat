Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Peshat is a camera-to-translation utility for iOS and Android. This policy describes what the app does with your data. The short version: your scanned text and images are processed on your device and are never transmitted to a server we control.

What we collect

Nothing. Peshat has no user accounts, no analytics service, no backend, and no database of any kind. We do not receive, store, or process any information about you or your device.

Camera

The app uses your device's camera to scan text. Captured images are processed entirely on your device by Apple's VisionKit (on iOS) or Google's ML Kit Document Scanner and Text Recognition (on Android). The images are not uploaded, not stored by the app after translation, and not sent to us or to any third party.

Translation

Translation is performed on your device using Google's ML Kit Translation. The text to be translated is never sent to a remote translation service.

Advertising

The free version of Peshat displays banner ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the following information to serve and measure advertisements:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • Device type, operating system version, and language
  • General location derived from IP address (country level)
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. See Google's advertising policies for details.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, Peshat asks for tracking permission the first time you encounter an ad. If you decline:

  • AdMob serves non-personalized ads only
  • Your IDFA is not accessed
  • Ads still appear, but are not tailored to your activity across other apps

If you allow, AdMob may use your IDFA to serve personalized ads. You can change this decision at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Removing ads

The app offers a single one-time purchase that permanently removes ads. This purchase is processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. Your purchase status is stored locally on your device.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or phone number
  • No account, login, or profile
  • No location data beyond what AdMob derives from IP
  • No contacts, photos, files, or other app data
  • No scanned text or images
  • No usage telemetry, crash reports, or analytics

Children

Peshat is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app displays ads, which are not personalized for users under the age of consent.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect data, there is nothing to retain or delete on our side. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to their policies. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, including your ad-removal purchase status (which can be restored from the App Store or Google Play).

International transfers

AdMob may process ad-related data in jurisdictions outside your own, including the United States. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will update. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the address on the support page.
